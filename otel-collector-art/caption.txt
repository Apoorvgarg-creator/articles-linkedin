You open the tracing bill and the busiest endpoint is /healthz.

Twenty health-check spans for every five real checkouts. Worse, those checkout spans are quietly shipping customer emails to a third-party vendor.

You don't fix that in app code. You fix it in the OpenTelemetry Collector, now a CNCF graduated project.

Issue #1 of my Wednesday OSS series opens up the piece inside that makes it work: the fan-out. One receiver, two pipelines. The vendor gets 5 clean spans. You still count all 25.

Plus the one-line config mistake that leaks a bearer token with zero warnings.

Runnable Go demo, no Docker needed. Link in the newsletter.
